01 · WHO WE ARE

More than a robot.
We're engineers of inspiration.

Team Tesseract is a high-achieving FIRST Tech Challenge team of fourteen students from Nashua, New Hampshire — designing, building and programming championship-caliber robots since 2022.

We qualified for the FIRST World Championship in Houston, claimed the Inspire Award for our 953 hours of outreach, and were ranked #32 out of 8,000+ teams worldwide for scoring ability. But our mission goes beyond competition.

We exist to advance STEM education, mentor the next generation of engineers, and prove that a group of students with a shop full of metal and a vision can change a community.

“FIRST lets us blend the thrill of competitive sports with the rigors of STEM education.”

— Jason Bottino, Team Captain

04 · ANAKATA (V3)

Meet Anakata.
Our Decode 2025–26 robot.

◆

Built for Every Angle

A 360° rotating turret, adjustable 30–60° hood, custom flywheels, and jam-resistant vector intake wheels make ANAKATA a versatile artifact scorer.

⬡

18 Artifacts, Then More

Its autonomous routine scores 18 artifacts; in driver control, ANAKATA can rapid-fire three artifacts at a time on the move, then park compactly in the Base Zone.

✦

Designed to Iterate

After rebuilding twice in under two months, the team built ANAKATA in 25 days. The V3 side-by-side intake replaces complex internal sorting, eliminating jams for a 223-point solo scoring capacity.
